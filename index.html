How custom orders work

From idea to delivery in four steps.

  1. Send your idea

    A photo, a sketch with measurements, the broken part itself, or a 3D file. No file is fine.

    STL · 3MF · OBJ · STEP

    Have an STL? Get an instant estimate.

  2. Get a quote

    Quotes are free. Price is based on the material, how long it takes to print, and any finishing work.

  3. We print it

    Your job goes in the queue on the printer that suits it best. Typical turnaround is within a week.

  4. Delivery or pickup

    We deliver locally, or you can grab it at the market. Pay with Square when you get it. No deposit on small jobs.

    Questions
    
    
    Good to know before you order.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    How much does a custom print cost?
    Every job is quoted. Price depends on the material, how long the print takes, and any finishing like sanding or assembly. There's a minimum charge of $15 per order. Quotes are free and there's no obligation.
    
    
    How long does it take?
    Most small jobs are ready within a week. Big or multi-part prints take longer. We'll give you a date with your quote.
    
    
    Can you copy a broken part?
    Usually, yes. Bring the part or send clear photos with measurements in millimetres. If a printed copy won't be strong enough for the job, we'll tell you before we print it.
    
    
    Will a printed part hold up in my truck or outside?
    It depends on the material. Standard PLA softens in a hot vehicle, so for dashboards, engine bays or outdoor use we print in PETG or ASA, or their carbon fibre versions when a part needs to be extra stiff.
    3D printed parts aren't meant for safety-critical or load-bearing uses such as brakes, steering, lifting or child seats.
    
    
    Can you print characters, logos or team crests?
    We print our own designs, licensed files, and logos you own or have permission to use. We can't reproduce trademarked characters or brands for resale.
    
    
    What if I don't have a 3D file?
    That's normal. Most people bring an idea, a sketch or a photo. We can design simple parts from measurements. Design time may be quoted separately.
