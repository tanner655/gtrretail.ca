How it works

Three steps. Then you forget about it.

No nagging, and no pings when you just drive past. Just one reminder when you actually stop at the store.

  1. Save your stores

    BagBuddy finds the real stores near you: Safeway, Co-op, Walmart, No Frills, the local market.

  2. Say where the bags are

    Tell BagBuddy where your bags are: in the car, at home, or already with you.

  3. Pull in, get one nudge

    Stop at the store and get one reminder. Tap "Got them" and you're done.

Questions

Good questions.

When can I get it?

BagBuddy is coming to Android first, on Google Play. iPhone comes after. Follow us on Instagram to hear the day it goes live. You can try the web version in your browser right now.

Does BagBuddy track where I go?

No. Your live location for arrival reminders is checked on your phone and isn't sent to our servers. The full details are in our privacy policy.

Will it ping me every time I drive past a store?

No. You get one reminder when you stop at a store you saved, and nothing after 10 pm.

Can my family use it too?

Yes. One Premium plan covers up to 4 people at home, with shared lists, so whoever goes to the store gets the reminder.

Who makes BagBuddy?

BagBuddy is made by GTR (Good Time Retail) Inc., a small Alberta company in Grande Prairie. We also run GTR Retail, which makes 3D prints and custom swag.
