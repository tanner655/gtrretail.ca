The problem

A busy booth runs on small steps.

Find the item, find its stock, measure, ring it up, put everything back. Off-the-shelf racks work, but they're one size, generic, and they make you do those steps over and over. We design displays that cut steps, travel well and look like your brand.

How it works

Prototype first. No guessing.

  1. Tell us about your boothWhat you sell, how many styles, your table and your vehicle. Photos help.
  2. We design itRenders in your colours and a clear quote before anything is printed.
  3. Try a prototypeA small test piece loaded with your own stock, so you know it works before you commit. The prototype cost comes off your order.
  4. We build the full setUsually 2 to 3 weeks after you approve the prototype.
  5. Set up and sellDelivered ready for your next market, and covered by our 6-month guarantee.
